## Direction contract

THESIS: Every account is an instrument of value — engraved, serialised, stamped when settled. Refuses the white-card KPI dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Pale security-tint ground (#EDF2EF) / night intaglio (#0A111C); intaglio-navy ink; one brand blue for action; green/red only for direction. Hairline engraved rules, 8–14px instrument corners, guilloché field behind the one certified figure, perforations, serial numerals, circular cancellation stamps.

STORY: The owner sees who owes whom, trusts the figure on sight, records or settles in seconds, and watches the stamp land.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Person screen — identity, then the instrument panel: serial line, position engraved large over guilloché, "owes you", workspace equivalent; perforation; counterfoil with the opening balance; labelled Record credit / Record debit / Settle; tabs; ledger spine of serialised stubs.

FORM: Counterfoil & Seal, grounded candidate 7, seed 6af229e3. Signature: the cancellation stamp landing on settlement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
